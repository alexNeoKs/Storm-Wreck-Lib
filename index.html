A GUIDE FOR THE GAME MASTER

Welcome to the Archive

Explore: drag to rotate the camera, scroll to zoom, and right-drag to pan. Use Battle map to see the long entrance bridge and central platform from above.

Move miniatures: select a hero on the left, an enemy on the right, or any miniature in 3D. Click a highlighted square to travel up to six orthogonal tiles. Occupied squares, the statue and the four podiums block movement.

Spawn enemies: click Spawn next to a creature, then click a free square on either the bridge or main platform. Click the enemy to move it or select Remove selected to delete it. You can summon multiple copies.

Narrate: the four podiums are purely visual. Use Success and Failure to flash a large message to the table when you decide an outcome. There is no built-in puzzle.

Display: hide the header using − and restore it with Show title. Keys 1–4 select party members; Escape cancels enemy placement. This is one shared DM-controlled screen, not live multiplayer.
